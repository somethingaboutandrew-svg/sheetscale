PlanMark Support

PDF markup and calibrated measurement for plan sheets. Your files stay on your device.

Get help

  • Report a problem or ask a question: open an issue on GitHub (free GitHub account needed). Please include your macOS version, PlanMark version (PlanMark menu > About PlanMark) and, if you can share it, a PDF that shows the problem.
  • Known issues and fixes: github.com/somethingaboutandrew-svg/planmark/issues
  • Release notes: github.com/somethingaboutandrew-svg/planmark/releases

Getting started

  1. Open a PDF or image with ⌘O, by dragging it onto the window, or with Finder's Open With.
  2. Set the scale. Press K and click both ends of a known length (a scale bar or a dimension), then type its real length. Or pick a standard scale such as 1" = 50' in the Scale panel.
  3. Measure. Length 1, polylength 2, perimeter 3, area 4, count 5. Double-click or press Enter to finish a shape. Totals appear in the Markups panel and export to CSV.
  4. Mark up with clouds, callouts, text, highlights, shapes and pens; organise them on layers you can hide or lock.
  5. Save with ⌘S: keep markups editable, or flatten them into the page. Both keep PDF layers.
  6. Combine PDFs and Reduce File Size losslessly from the File menu.

Press ? in the app for every keyboard shortcut.

Frequently asked

  • Are my files uploaded? No. Everything runs on your Mac. See the privacy policy.
  • What can I open? PDF files and images (PNG, JPG, TIFF including multi-page, GIF, BMP, WebP). Images become PDF pages.
  • How accurate are measurements? Measurements are computed from the PDF's own coordinates and the scale you set, and are as accurate as the drawing and your calibration. Always check a known dimension after calibrating.
  • Can other apps read my markups? Yes. Editable saves use standard PDF annotations and measurement dictionaries; flattened saves draw the markups into the page.
  • Large files? PlanMark is tested with combined 300-page reports of about 125 MB.
  • Where are recent files kept? In PlanMark's private storage on your Mac. Use Clear under Recent on the start screen to remove them.
