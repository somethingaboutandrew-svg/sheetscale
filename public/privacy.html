Privacy Policy

SheetScale for Mac, Windows and the web. Effective 31 October 2026.

The short version

SheetScale does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Your files never leave your device. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your documents

  • PDFs and images you open in SheetScale are read, edited, combined, reduced and saved entirely on your own computer.
  • Nothing you open is uploaded to any server. SheetScale has no server that could receive it.
  • To let you pick up where you left off, SheetScale keeps copies of open and recently closed documents, your markups and your settings in the app's private storage on your device (in the Mac app, inside its sandbox container). Use Clear under Recent on the start screen to remove stored copies of closed files, or delete the app to remove everything.
  • Files are saved only where you choose in the Save dialog.

Network use

  • The Mac App Store version makes no network connections of its own. It does not have the macOS permission to make outgoing network connections.
  • The web version is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading it downloads the app's own files; after that it works offline. GitHub may keep standard web-server logs (such as IP address) when the site is visited; see GitHub's privacy statement at https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. SheetScale itself adds no analytics, cookies or trackers.
  • Links you click (for example to this page or the support page) open in your web browser.

Data collected

None. SheetScale does not collect contact information, identifiers, usage data, diagnostics, location or any other category of data. Apple's App Store may provide the developer with aggregated, anonymous sales and download statistics under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

SheetScale collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/somethingaboutandrew-svg/sheetscale/issues or see the support page.
